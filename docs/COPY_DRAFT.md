## 1. 语气原则 · Voice

| 原则 | Principle |
| --- | --- |
| **具体**：每句话都基于这只狗自己的数据或今天的实际情况，不说放之四海皆准的套话。 | **Specific**: every line comes from this dog's own data or today's real conditions — no generic advice. |
| **不诊断**：只描述"和平时不一样"，需要时建议问兽医，从不猜病名。 | **Never diagnose**: describe what's different from usual; suggest a vet when it matters; never name a condition. |
| **不打分、不内疚**：只累加做过的事，不显示"完成率 40%"。漏记就漏记了。 | **No grades, no guilt**: count what you did, never "40% complete." A missed log is just a missed log. |
| **先给理由，再给动作**：说清楚为什么提，再给一个现在就能做的小事。 | **Reason, then one action**: say why it's worth mentioning, then one small thing you can do now. |
| **短**：一条卡片两句话以内。 | **Short**: two sentences per card, max. |
| **平静的一天也值得说一句**：一切正常时，就说一切正常。 | **Quiet days count**: when everything's normal, say so. |

## 3. 每日卡片 · Daily cards

每天大约 2–3 张，分四种。
About 2–3 cards a day, of four kinds.

### 3.1 观察：和平时不一样 · Noticed: something's different

基于这只狗过去几周的日常节奏。数据少于一周时不出这类卡片。
Based on this dog's own routine over the past few weeks. Not shown until there's about a week of logs.

| 中文 | English |
| --- | --- |
| {name} 今天到现在只记了 1 次便便，平时这个时间一般已经 2 次了。可能只是忘了记，晚点留意一下就好。 | Only 1 poop logged for {name} so far today — usually it's 2 by now. Might just be unlogged; worth a glance later. |
| 这三天 {name} 每天多喝了大约一碗水。天热的话很正常；如果天不热，可以再观察几天。 | {name}'s been going through about one extra bowl of water a day for 3 days. Totally normal in hot weather — if it's not hot, keep an eye on it for a few more days. |
| {name} 连着两天晚饭都剩了一些。偶尔挑食很常见；如果持续好几天，或者精神也不太好，可以问问兽医。 | {name} has left some dinner two nights in a row. Picky days happen — if it lasts several days or they seem low on energy, check in with your vet. |
| 最近三次梳毛都记了"掉毛多"。金毛换季掉毛很正常，这段时间每天梳几分钟会轻松很多。 | Your last 3 brushings were all "heavy shedding." That's normal seasonal shedding for a Golden — a few minutes a day makes it much easier. |

来自计划，按你实际上一次完成的时间推算，而不是按日历硬算。
From your plans, counted from when you actually last did it — not a fixed calendar.

### 3.3 小贴士：结合今天 · Tip for today

结合季节、天气、犬种和最近的记录。没有关联的贴士不出。
Tied to the season, weather, breed, and recent logs. No tip without a reason.

| 中文 | English |

## 4. 我们不会这么说 · What we won't say

| 不说 · Not this | 原因 · Why |
| --- | --- |
| "{name} 可能有肾脏问题。" · "{name} may have kidney issues." | 诊断，而且会吓人 · Diagnosis, and scary |
| "本周护理完成率仅 40%，继续加油！" · "Only 40% of care done this week. Keep going!" | 打分，让人内疚 · Grading and guilt |
| "夏季注意防暑。" · "Watch out for heat in summer." | 套话，没有依据 · Generic, no data behind it |
| "心丝虫预防晚了 5 天！" · "Heartworm prevention is 5 days late!" | 催促；只说距离上次多少天 · Pushy — just say how many days since the last dose |
| "体重与上月持平。"（上月根本没有记录）· "Weight unchanged from last month." (with no data last month) | 没有数据就下结论 · A conclusion without data |

## 7. 想请你们回答的问题 · Questions for you

1. 整体语气让你觉得温暖吗？还是太啰嗦、太黏人？
   Does the tone feel warm — or too chatty?
2. 每天 2–3 张卡片合适吗？你会更想要几张？
   Is 2–3 cards a day right? More? Fewer?
3. 你喂饭用什么计量（杯、克、罐）？知道自家水碗大概多大吗？
   How do you measure food (cups, grams, cans)? Do you roughly know your water bowl's size?
4. **驱虫**：现在按覆盖范围记录（体内寄生虫 / 跳蚤和蜱虫 / 心丝虫预防），每一类可以选"按时用药"或"定期检查、听医生的"。这和你们家的做法对得上吗？
   **Parasite prevention**: you log what each dose covers (intestinal worms / fleas & ticks / heartworm prevention), and for each one you choose "on a schedule" or "regular tests, following your vet." Does that match how your family does it?
5. 第 2 节里，哪些你真的会每天点？哪些永远不会碰？
   In section 2, which would you actually tap every day? Which would you never touch?
6. 英文里有没有哪句读起来不自然，或者不像美国人会说的话？
   Any English lines that sound off or not like something people actually say?
7. 年度回顾里，你最想看到什么？
   What would make you happiest to see in a year-in-review?
8. 你的狗属于哪一组？默认的梳毛、洗澡频率符合你的实际习惯吗？
   Which group is your dog in? Do the default brushing and bath frequencies match what you actually do?
